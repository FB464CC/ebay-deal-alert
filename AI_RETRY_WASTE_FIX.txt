2026-09-10 — Chosen design
- Cap at 3 completed AI_NO_PRICE outcomes per item_id: the initial attempt plus two genuine retries. After one or two misses the item remains unseen/retryable exactly as before; the third makes it final and calls `mark_seen()`. In this real 22-failure incident that would spend 3 calls and avoid the next 19. The later 23rd-call recovery only proved the item was worth $75 and produced GOLF_REJECT, so no delivered deal would have been lost here.
- Track the integer on the existing `ai_pending` row in seen_items.db rather than add a new table/file. Budget-starved rows and total provider failures do not increment it; only the distinct completed-call AI_NO_PRICE disposition does. Any other completed outcome already reaches a final disposition and `mark_seen()` deletes the pending row, which naturally resets the consecutive sequence.
- Permanently `mark_seen()` the exact item_id at the cap. This is the smallest mechanism and matches all other final dispositions. Marketplace relists normally receive a new item_id; an edit to the same live Facebook ID is still the same candidate, and neither the existing fingerprint (title/platform only) nor Scout payload exposes a reliable “photos improved” revision identity. Building speculative revision-state machinery would be larger and less truthful than the evidence supports.
