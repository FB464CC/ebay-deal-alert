Executive conclusion
--------------------
The preliminary inventory hypothesis is mostly right, but incomplete.

- Over 23.7 days the bot saw 13,145 golf rows and AI-checked 3,998. Only TWO
  rows were simultaneously a genuine 2012+ named model, a coherent playable
  set, non-left-handed, undamaged, non-counterfeit, and at least 25% under the
  AI estimate. One was only 28% under and was correctly rejected by the
  configured 50% steal bar. The other was 69% under and was delivered.
- The one delivered modern target was eBay item 237080223566, `Callaway Rogue
  Irons 5-P A Stiff Flex Steel Right Handed Excellent Condition`, $140.45
  logged landed versus a $450 estimate. It was logged delivered at
  2026-09-25T15:46:19.053751Z. eBay's own page records an auction end at
  2026-09-25T15:47:40Z. The alert therefore arrived only 80.95 seconds before
  the auction ended. Algorithmically that is a hit; for a personal buyer it is
  reasonably described as unusable.
  https://www.ebay.com/itm/237080223566
- So the important number is not literally zero: it is 2 qualifying >=25%
  candidates in 23.7 days, only 1 at the configured >=50% bar, and 0 with a
  practically useful buying window. This is "near zero" inventory in the only
  sense that matters to the owner.
- The referee is biased: 13 of 15 audited AI resale estimates are below the
  midpoint of real eBay sold comparables, with a median signed error (AI minus
  sold-range midpoint) of -$59.99. That bias can suppress genuinely good
  15%-40%-under-market sets. It does not explain away most rejections as hidden
  steals: most asks were still around market once shipping and actual comps are
  considered.
- Sources are not equivalent. Official eBay produced one valid >=25% modern
  set but no >=50% set; eBay-scraped produced the sole >=50% hit; ShopGoodwill
  produced zero clean modern sets even 1% under its estimate; Facebook/Scout
  produced one clean modern set, only 5.4% under; OfferUp produced no rows.
- The bot's gates are not the primary reason for weeks of silence. The
  combination of scarce inventory, a 50%-under requirement, estimate bias, and
  one catastrophically late auction alert is. Another narrow junk/handedness
  gate tweak will not manufacture supply.

Recommendation to the owner
---------------------------
Do not spend another week tweaking the handedness, vintage, or set-shape gates.
Choose explicitly between:

- rare true steals (1 algorithmic hit / 0 timely hits in 23.7 days), or
- a $200 absolute ceiling (6 historical candidates, shown above), or
- materially better local/private-seller acquisition with freshness, radius,
  and auction lead-time treated as first-class requirements.

The estimate system should also be recalibrated against sold comps before it is
trusted as the sole price referee: median error -$59.99 is not roughly
calibrated. But correcting that alone will mostly surface good/fair $180-$300
sets, not manufacture frequent 50%-under steals.
