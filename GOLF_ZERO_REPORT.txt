Checkpoint 3 - code-path findings and market calibration
--------------------------------------------------------
- config.json currently has 209 searches, 125 enabled, and 43 enabled
  golf-equipment searches (all fast). Price caps: 12x $300, 9x $220, 4x $200,
  and the remainder $40-$160. All 43 enable ShopGoodwill and Facebook; eBay is
  polled independently for every enabled search by design.
- Configured AI throughput is 12 shared calls/run, a golf soft ceiling of 6
  shared calls/run, plus 1 isolated eBay-scrape call/run. The soft cap is not a
  golf reservation; it prevents golf crowd-out while non-golf waits.
- ebay_deal_alert.py:1964-2038 honors explicit category metadata first and routes
  inferred club/set/bag queries to golf-equipment before the apparel "golf" branch.
  Classification is working: production has 14,666 correctly categorized records.
- ebay_deal_alert.py:2785-2879 exempts ordinary eBay from local relevance checks
  and accepts a non-eBay title when any non-stopword query token matches. This
  admits exact production junk such as golf-club neckties, iron patio furniture,
  a cast-iron frying-pan set, headcovers, ferrules, organizers, and single
  replacement irons. ebay_deal_alert.py:2948-2955 then sends every surviving golf
  title to REVIEW; there is no cheap golf product/type gate before paid vision.
- ebay_deal_alert.py:3635-3719 asks vision whether the listing is a playable first
  set and explicitly says a bag alone or single club must return false.
- ebay_deal_alert.py:4191-4232 requires golf_is_playable_first_set=true for every
  golf-equipment search. That makes eight intentionally configured component hunts
  (three drivers, two putters, one wedge, two stand bags) structurally unable to
  alert. Production proves the collision: eight reviewed component-search rows
  were rejected for playability, including a genuine right-handed Odyssey White
  Hot putter at $29.15 and genuine stand bags/drivers.
- The same gate unconditionally requires brand_claims_confirmed. The AI prompt
  defines that as matching a brand/model claim in the title and requires false
  when markings/claims are absent. Therefore a generic but legitimate "Golf clubs"
  or "Golf bag" title has no claim it can confirm and can be blocked even when the
  AI identifies a safe brand. The log only exposes the first failing gate, so this
  hidden co-failure is not measurable from old rows.
- Handedness conservatism is not the primary bottleneck: only 9/149 checked rows
  stopped on unknown handedness (6.0%) and none stopped as explicitly left-handed.
  Keeping fail-closed right-handedness for actual clubs is defensible; bags do not
  have handedness and should not be subjected to that club-only requirement.
- Price caps are not the observed bottleneck: zero BELOW_MARGIN rows, only 13
  near-search-cap rows across the wide window, and just one checked hard-$300-cap
  failure. Of checked candidates, 36 actually asked more than the AI's value.
  Current real eBay evidence supports keeping, not raising, the deal caps: a used
  Ping G400 4-PW sold at $440.99 and another at $549 (versus the bot's $220 cap);
  a used Odyssey White Hot XG #9 right-handed putter sold at $76.49 (versus the
  bot's $40 cap). Those caps represent genuine roughly-half-market steals. The
  live TaylorMade M2 set market is commonly ~$230-$500+, while the bot caps M2 at
  $220. Legitimate deals can exist below these thresholds (and the two production
  deliveries did), so raising them would lower deal quality without repairing the
  actual throughput/type mismatch.

Evidence-backed root causes before implementation:
1. Primary: AI-budget starvation caused by high-volume retry churn; 87.4% of
   unique candidates and 98.8% of attempt rows never reached AI.
2. Major contributor: golf has no deterministic product/type prefilter, so scarce
   vision calls and the pending queue are filled with obvious merchandise,
   accessories, furniture, and isolated replacement clubs.
3. Genuine logic bug: the universal playable-set rule contradicts eight explicit
   component searches, rejecting correct component deals by construction.
4. Hidden overly broad requirement: a generic listing with no seller brand/model
   claim is treated like an unverified claimed-brand listing.
5. Not causal: category detection works; per-search maximums are economically
   defensible; handedness ambiguity is a small minority rather than the total block.

Changes in ebay_deal_alert.py (final line numbers):
1. Lines 1892-2012: added explicit query-intent detection for the eight standalone
   component searches and a conservative golf title/type prefilter. It rejects
   only title-certain wrong items: non-club merchandise, obvious accessories,
   single/replacement clubs under a set-oriented query, component-query mismatches,
   and set queries with no multi-club/set evidence. Ambiguous titles still go to AI.
2. Lines 3395-3459: taught the final text sanity pass that a whole driver, putter,
   wedge, or stand bag is complete when that is what the saved search requested;
   accessory-only variants still reject.
3. Lines 3606-3836: passes the clean saved-search intent into the golf vision prompt
   and adds is_wanted_component plus brand_claims_present. The model must visually
   confirm the actual requested whole component, not a shaft/cover/grip/organizer.
4. Lines 4329-4382 (within is_blocked_by_steal_quality_gate, defined at 4170): a
   visually confirmed requested component may clear without pretending to be a
   playable set. Right-handedness remains fail-closed for every actual club; only
   stand bags skip the nonsensical handedness check. Brand confirmation is required
   when the seller made a brand/model claim; no claim is no longer treated as a
   mismatch. Missing new-model output still fails closed as legacy claims-present.
5. Lines 4864-4995: added GOLF_WRONG_ITEM and logs the individual golf AI gate inputs
   (playability, component match/type, handedness, claim presence/confirmation,
   identified brand, counterfeit, damage) so future evidence is not limited to the
   first gate reason that happened to return.
6. Lines 7037-7074: installed the title/type prefilter before scoring/paid vision and
   logs every such disposition instead of silently discarding it.
7. Lines 7622-7638: merges the new vision fields while preserving a missing
   brand_claims_present field as None so malformed/old AI output cannot fail open.
