Progress 3 - actual threshold/gate semantics
--------------------------------------------
- compute_deal_rating() (final lines 4148-4180) uses the site-wide buckets:
  Steal >=70% under estimated resale; Great Deal >=50%; Good Deal >=30%; Fair
  >=10%; Marginal below 10%.
- Before this fix, the golf branch of is_blocked_by_steal_quality_gate() returned
  before the generic margin branch (final lines 4794-4839). It explicitly said resale
  margin is not a gate. Golf needs: a real AI check; non-blocked brand; landed
  price <= $300; landed price not ABOVE a provided resale estimate; playable
  set/useful partial or the requested standalone component; right-handedness for
  clubs; confirmed seller brand claims when present; no counterfeit signal; and
  no disqualifying damage. There is no hard resale-value floor, minimum-dollar
  savings, liquidity gate, confidence gate, or 70% requirement for golf.
- Before this fix, the merge path skipped the generic second opinion for golf and
  skipped compute_deal_rating() for golf. Consequently a
  normal golf result has no deal_rating/discount_pct even when it has a sound
  resale estimate. send_alert() still sends it, but alert_urgency() (final lines
  5088-5111) sees no rating/discount and assigns the default priority-3 `eyes`
  tag. The eyeballs therefore mean "unrated/default priority", not "failed the
  gate" or "was not delivered". This is the precise presentation bug behind the
  review-only appearance.
- The $265 complete-set row proves the separate null-estimate behavior: a real AI
  check completed, confirmed a playable right-handed set and returned no usable
  estimate. Because golf deliberately treats pricing as optional, the gate passed
  and the row was delivered=true. It is not an incomplete AI call and not a render
  bug; the model abstained and send_alert() correctly had no estimate to render.

Progress 5 - bounded implementation
-----------------------------------
No price/search cap, saved search, category rule, AI budget, or $300 personal-use
cap was relaxed. config.json is unchanged.

Changes made in ebay_deal_alert.py:
1. Golf results with a usable estimate now run through the existing
   compute_deal_rating() buckets, so a genuine >=70% gap says Steal and receives
   urgent fire tagging, while 50-69% says Great Deal. Personal use no longer means
   "hide the price evidence".
2. The golf gate now requires a usable estimate and applies the same normal
   delivered-deal floor already used site-wide: Steal or Great Deal (>=50% under
   current resale value), plus non-low price confidence. This RAISES the prior
   effective golf floor from merely price<=value (0% savings); it does not lower a
   bar to manufacture alerts. It also keeps every prior playability, component,
   $300 cap, handedness, claim-verification, counterfeit, and damage check.
3. A completed golf AI call that returns null value now stays retry-eligible via
   the established "no AI price estimate" marker. The real $265 anonymous set can
   no longer be sent as a purported deal without any evidence it is one. It also
   receives a distinct AI_NO_PRICE disposition (rather than masquerading as
   NO_AI_BUDGET) while retaining the same retry marker.
4. The golf vision prompt now tells the model to match exact club count/model,
   flex, handedness, and condition to completed-sale value; not to collapse an old
   economy-tier matched set to near-zero purely for lacking prestige; and includes
   the dated Wilson/Warrior/Cleveland completed-market anchors above. The anchors
   are deliberately two-sided: Wilson is raised to real evidence, Warrior stays
   around $60, and Cleveland wedges are not inflated.
