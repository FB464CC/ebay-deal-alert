Part 1 — Findings (increment 1: materials, makers, and new-retail baseline)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Material distinction

* “Clay” in the casino/collector market means a proprietary composite formed
  by thermo/compression molding, not literal pottery clay. Classic Poker Chips
  says modern American-style clay has been a mixed composition for 75+ years
  and is pressed one chip at a time at 10,000 psi and 300°F. It explicitly says
  injection-molded chips advertised as clay are plastic, even when they contain
  clay-like filler. GPI's 2018 Form 10-K independently calls Paulson and Blue
  Chip thermo-compression products “clay chips” and distinguishes them from its
  injection-molded plastic lines.
  Sources: https://classicpokerchips.com/custom.html
           https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReports/PDF/NASDAQ_GPIC_2018.pdf

* Genuine ceramic is a separate acceptable path, not a subtype of
  compression-molded clay. BR Pro describes ceramic chips as a ceramic/high-
  density-polymer blend with full-face and edge artwork dye-sublimated into the
  blank rather than an inlay, sticker, or label; CHIPCO likewise says the image
  becomes part of the chip via high-heat transfer and can cover face and edge.
  Therefore the existing clay-only AI path does NOT implicitly cover ceramic:
  its `is_genuine_clay=true` plus embedded-inlay requirements necessarily reject
  the legitimate ceramic construction.
  Sources: https://brpropoker.com/pages/frequently-asked-questions
           https://www.chipco.com/chipco-faq

Maker/brand tier correction

* Keep Paulson, ASM, BCC/Blue Chip, CPC, and TR King/TRK as meaningful names for
  compression-molded clay. CPC documents that today's operation continues the
  historic Burt/ASM production lineage and says it is the only current maker
  offering genuine compression-molded clay to retail customers. GPI's filing
  confirms Paulson and Blue Chip as thermo-compression-molded lines. The current
  prompt's “GPIC” should not be presented as another maker tier: GPIC was the
  corporate owner (Gaming Partners International Corporation), while Paulson
  and Blue Chip were its clay brands. A collector history also identifies the
  Burt→ASM→CPC lineage, TRK, Paulson, and BCC as the principal compression-clay
  makers.
  Sources: https://classicpokerchips.com/about.html
           https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReports/PDF/NASDAQ_GPIC_2018.pdf
           https://www.pokerchipforum.com/threads/is-there-room-for-a-new-clay-chip-manufacturer-in-na-or-eu.79767/

* Do not add Bud Jones or B&G to the accepted material tier. They can be
  valuable casino-grade collectibles, but GPI's own filing classifies Bud
  Jones, B&G, and Dolphin American-style currencies as plastic injection-
  molded, often with a central metal piece. They are outside this buyer's stated
  clay-or-ceramic scope. “House of Cards” also should not be treated as a maker
  without an independently visible manufacturer/mold; the research found no
  manufacturer evidence supporting it as a material-authentication brand.
  Source: https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReports/PDF/NASDAQ_GPIC_2018.pdf

* Add genuine ceramic makers/lines as a parallel tier: CHIPCO, BR Pro Poker,
  SUN-FLY, Nevada Jacks, and SLOWPLAY's ceramic lines. CHIPCO identifies itself as the original ceramic-chip maker;
  BR Pro manufactures casino-quality ceramic sets in California; SUN-FLY's
  current catalog calls its POLYCHROM line pure ceramic with face-and-edge
  printing and no inserts/decals. Maker identity is supportive context only;
  the photos must still show the correct embedded full-surface print rather
  than a stickered injection-molded chip.
  Sources: https://www.chipco.com/
           https://brpropoker.com/pages/casino-quality-poker-chips
           https://www.sunflycasinochips.com/_files/ugd/245739_9b9694c637df4bafaccb5a9ee4877220.pdf
           https://brpropoker.com/collections/nevada-jacks
           https://www.slowplay.store/collections/all

Photo/listing tells for conservative AI triage

* Compression clay: look for a visibly impressed rim mold; actual contrasting
  clay edge inserts continuing through the rolling edge; an embedded inlay OR
  a legitimate foil hot stamp; and coherent wear, colors, mold, face finish,
  and spot pattern within each denomination. CPC states that the mold is the
  signature rim relief, that edge spots are contrasting clay pressed into the
  rim rather than paint/print, and that inlays are molded into the body rather
  than stickers. CPC also sells authentic hot-stamped clay, so “must have an
  inlay” is a false-negative rule and should be replaced with an accepted-face-
  finish path.
  Sources: https://classicpokerchips.com/custom.html
           https://classicpokerchips.com/stock/index.htm
           https://classicpokerchips.com/pokerchips/realclay/hotstamps.htm

* Ceramic: look for a single-composition blank with dye-sublimated, continuous
  full-face/edge artwork and no peelable label, separate inlay, or metal insert.
  Do not demand clay edge inserts or a clay mold. BR Pro says its standard 39mm
  ceramics are about 9.5g, while the artwork is heat-pressed into originally
  white blanks; a small amount of edge-print registration variation can be
  normal and must not be treated like mismatched clay inserts.
  Sources: https://brpropoker.com/pages/frequently-asked-questions
           https://brpropoker.com/

* Negative tells: seller-only “clay,” “casino grade,” “clay composite,” or
  “clay feel” claims; 11.5g–14g weight marketing; a central metal slug/iron core;
  injection seams/nubs; peelable center labels; uniformly mass-produced dice/
  suit patterns; and shrink-wrapped stacks in a generic home-game kit. GPI
  confirms that its injection-molded lines commonly use a central metal piece;
  CPC says every injection-molded chip is plastic even with clay-like filler;
  and live eBay/OfferUp examples show 13g–14g “clay” claims on metal-core or
  composite case sets. Weight is only a warning signal, never proof by itself.
  Sources: https://www.annualreports.com/HostedData/AnnualReports/PDF/NASDAQ_GPIC_2018.pdf
           https://classicpokerchips.com/custom.html
           https://www.ebay.com/shop/poker-chip-set-with-case?_nkw=poker+chip+set+with+case
           https://offerup.com/item/detail/43db49af-8e29-33f8-aa7e-bd654105b3d6

* A case is discovery context, not authentication. Both a $59.99 China-origin
  set and common 14g Claytec-style kits use aluminum cases, while real antique
  clay can appear in walnut/wooden boxes. Ask the model to count racks/stacks
  when possible: casino terminology is 20 chips per barrel and 100 per rack,
  which helps distinguish a playable 300/500-chip set from a costly handful.
  Sources: https://www.ebay.com/itm/176978568804
           https://www.ebay.com/itm/173323403144
           https://www.ebay.com/itm/406931327904
           https://www.apachepokerchips.com/product-category/barrels-and-racks/

Part 2 — Changes applied and why
--------------------------------

1. Re-enabled every existing poker-chip search. No current query was dropped:
   all five map to evidenced seller language, and the photo gate is the right
   place to reject “clay” marketing noise.

   Existing searches enabled:
   - casino poker chips set (`poker-set`)
   - paulson poker chips (`poker-paulson`)
   - vintage casino chip set (`poker-vintage-set`)
   - clay poker chips lot (`poker-clay-lot`)
   - poker chip set vintage (`poker-set-vintage`)

2. Added 13 fast, enabled poker-chip searches at the same $150 maximum:
   - ceramic poker chips set (`poker-ceramic-set`): broad ceramic discovery
   - casino chips lot (`poker-lot`): under-described/mixed estate lots
   - chipco poker chips (`poker-chipco`): genuine ceramic maker
   - nevada jacks poker chips (`poker-nevada-jacks`): established ceramic line
   - sunfly poker chips (`poker-sunfly`): genuine ceramic maker spelling likely
     to occur in marketplace titles
   - br pro poker chips (`poker-br-pro`): genuine ceramic maker
   - asm poker chips (`poker-asm`): retired compression-clay provenance
   - classic poker chips (`poker-classic`): current retail compression clay
   - cpc poker chips (`poker-cpc`): acronym spelling of Classic Poker Chips
   - bcc poker chips (`poker-bcc`): retired Blue Chip compression clay
   - tr king poker chips (`poker-tr-king`): retired collectible clay maker
   - hot stamp poker chips (`poker-hot-stamp`): authentic non-inlay clay path
   - top hat cane poker chips (`poker-top-hat-cane`): common Paulson mold wording

3. Retained implicit eBay coverage and expanded only the poker rows' auxiliary
   platform scopes from ShopGoodwill+Facebook to
   ShopGoodwill+Vinted+OfferUp+Facebook. No `ebay` string was added because the
   bot's main Browse API loop already consumes all enabled saved searches.
   Grailed, Poshmark, and Depop were deliberately not added for the researched
   noise/budget reasons above.

4. Kept `POKER_CHIPS_MAX_PRICE = 150` and every poker row's `max_price = 150`.
   Current 500-chip genuine ceramic retail/sold comparisons make this a real
   discount threshold; the gate continues to treat it only as a maximum landed
   cost worthy of human research, not an automatic appraisal.

5. Corrected and expanded the AI photo prompt:
   - separated compression-molded clay from dye-sublimated ceramic;
   - corrected GPIC/GPI from “maker” to corporate provenance;
   - retained Paulson, BCC/Blue Chip, Burt/ASM/CPC, and TRK clay context;
   - added CHIPCO, BR Pro, SUN-FLY, Nevada Jacks, and SLOWPLAY ceramic context;
   - explicitly excluded Bud Jones/B&G injection-molded plastic for this buyer;
   - stopped treating roughness alone as mandatory clay proof;
   - added mold/through-edge clay-insert, metal-core/injection-nub, ceramic
     embedded-print, replaced-label, and chip-count guidance;
   - allowed authentic hot-stamped compression clay rather than requiring every
     genuine clay chip to have an inlay;
   - preserved conservative unknown/false behavior and the prohibition on AI
     price/resale judgments.

6. Added and wired three AI fields used by the gate:
   `is_genuine_ceramic`, `has_hot_stamp_not_sticker`, and
   `ceramic_design_embedded_not_sticker`. The gate now accepts exactly:
   - visually confirmed compression clay with molded inlay OR authentic hot
     stamp; or
   - visually confirmed ceramic with an embedded/dye-sublimated design.
   Both still require coherent edge treatment and no suspected alteration.
   ABS, metal-core, composite, sticker/label, unknown, over-$150, and unverified
   results remain blocked.

7. Extended the natural existing poker test classes for ceramic acceptance,
   missing-embedded-design rejection, hot-stamp acceptance, updated schema and
   prompt language, all 18 enabled searches, and the expanded platform scope.
   No non-poker behavior/configuration was changed.
