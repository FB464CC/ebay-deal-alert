## Checkpoint 2 — cap provenance and cost evidence

- `GEMINI_CALL_LIMIT=12` is a local scheduling cap, not a documented DeepSeek hard limit. Repository history says it was raised 6→12 "for faster watch coverage"; an earlier Gemini-only era instead reduced an 8-per-run pacing cap to 3 after its approximately 1,000-RPD free quota exhausted early. The current primary is DeepSeek, with Gemini as fallback.
- Official DeepSeek documentation currently lists a 2,500-concurrent-request limit for Flash and token billing (rather than a 12-per-run limit); images are billed as tokens. Official Gemini documentation says quotas are project/tier/model specific (RPM, TPM, and RPD), so this checkout cannot establish a safe Gemini-fallback increase without the account's AI Studio quota view.
- The checked-in paid ledger is already full: UTC September has `reserved_usd=10.000000000000188` across 2,232 calls, exactly matching the configured `$10` monthly safety cap and `$0.005` vision reservation. The current log observed 219 completed AI checks in roughly 15.5 hours (about 339/day if sustained). At the existing conservative reservation, that rate alone implies about `$1.70/day` or `$51/month`; there is no paid-cost headroom to raise the global cap without an explicit larger budget. A pool increase would primarily press the unknown Gemini fallback quota after the DeepSeek ledger rejects new paid calls.
- Preliminary conclusion: preserve the total shared cap of 12; solve the demonstrated shared-pool inequity with a small poker reservation, not an unjustified total-cost increase.

## Checkpoint 4 — staleness decision (fresh replay at remote content SHA 6b3978a4f5d1)

- A later fresh pull contained 704 rows through `2026-09-10T17:00:15Z`, including 149 poker rows and 68 `STALE_LISTING` rows. Source/age breakdown: 59 eBay-scraped (43 at observed age 1 day, 15 at 2 days, 1 at 3 days), 2 OfferUp (observed age 1 day), 6 Vinted (source ages 4, 38, 39, 40, 83, 251 days), and one official eBay row (6 days). The scrape/OfferUp values are honest `ai_pending` lower bounds where no source post timestamp exists; Vinted/eBay have source-age signals.
- Query classification is decisively against a poker exception: 46/68 (67.6%) came from direct maker/provenance queries, 15/68 (22.1%) from direct material/self-description queries, and only 7/68 (10.3%) from broad generic/estate discovery queries. The two stale `casino chips lot` rows were Christmas ornaments and a 50-chip Bakelite lot, not a qualifying >=100-chip clay/ceramic set. The generic stale rows include plastic, Bicycle, toy, and Cardinal sets; the direct cohorts are predominantly Cardinal, explicit composite/metal-core, sample, or 25/50-chip noise. No stale row has sold-comps evidence, and each was suppressed because fresh/unknown-age candidates were available.
- The historical poker research supports broad discovery in principle, but supplies no good-find age distribution: the few historical poker deliveries were generic/single-chip examples with null age, not confirmed qualifying sets. The requested old `POKER_PREMIUM.txt` and `POKER_QUALITY.txt` are absent; the relevant retained history is `POKER_CHIPS_RESEARCH.txt` and remote commits.
- A 3-day poker override would re-admit 60/68 current rows (88.2%); a 7-day override would re-admit 63/68. That is a large, evidenced reintroduction of noise into the exact budget this task must protect, without a verified-good counterexample. Decision: leave the universal one-day staleness behavior unchanged for poker and every other category. Consequently there is no category override or override-only regression to add.

## Checkpoint 5 — targeted allocation implementation

Implemented `POKER_CHIPS_SHARED_AI_SOFT_CAP: 2` in `config.json` and passed it into
`_apply_shared_ai_category_fairness()` from `run()`. The shared-pool allocator now
keeps bounded golf and poker shares in the executable prefix, then fills remaining
slots by the original priority ordering. Fresh shared-lane evidence supported a
2-slot poker share: 51 poker rows (35 unique), versus 166 golf rows (124 unique) and
118 watches rows, with enabled-search counts of 18, 43, and 29 respectively. This
preserves golf's existing 6-slot share and leaves four of the 12 shared slots available
to the non-reserved priority pool.

The total `GEMINI_CALL_LIMIT` remains 12. It is a scheduler/cost-control cap rather
than a published fixed provider quota, but the local paid-AI ledger was already at
$10.00 for 2,232 reservations this month. Raising it is not safe headroom. The
isolated eBay-scrape lane remains 1 because it protects the shared pool from scraper
floods, and Scout remains 1 because 132 current golf Scout budget misses were retry
churn across only three listings rather than 132 distinct opportunities.

Added focused unit and integration regressions proving that a poker candidate reaches
an actual AI call while golf consumes its own reserved share. No staleness override
was added: the fresh stale-row evidence did not support one.
