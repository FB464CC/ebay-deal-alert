SHOPGOODWILL GOLF AUCTIONS: PRICE-AT-ALERT vs FINAL PRICE, AND REAL SHIPPING
=============================================================================
Measured 2026-10-05 from ShopGoodwill's own item-detail API: 1,785 closed golf
auctions the bot had observed 2026-09-08..2026-10-05, each with its full bid
history (bidComplete: bidTime + displayed itemPrice). All times Pacific (PDT).
Method: for every auction, rebuild the displayed price at N minutes before close
from the bid history and divide the FINAL price by it. >=1.5x / >=2x columns are
the share of auctions that rose by at least that much.

FINDING 1 - 'prices doubled after the final bid' is NOT the typical pattern on
ShopGoodwill. Uncontested auctions (0 bids) never moved at all (p90 x1.00). Contested
ones have a real upper tail but a median near x1.0-1.06; ~1-6% reach double.
The doubling cases are real but are the tail: e.g. Ping G10/i5 set seen $31.89 with
11 min left closed $87.00 (x2.73, 11 bids); TaylorMade M2 lot seen $142 closed $237
(x1.67, 34 bids). eBay close behaviour is NOT measured here (eBay returns 403 to
scripts); 3 verified eBay closings from the earlier audit were x1.0, x1.40 (21 bids,
3 min left) and x2.14 (5 bids, 1 min left), too few to generalise.

FINDING 2 - the flat $13.50 shipping assumption understated golf landed cost.
ShopGoodwill's CalculateShipping endpoint, queried for 14 real brand iron/club sets
spanning 1-50 lb shipped to ZIP 29201 (Columbia, SC), total shipping+handling:
  $16.69 $19.17 $23.22 $23.56 $15.06 $12.87 $14.11 $30.77 $19.43 $18.71 $29.01 $17.19 $22.82 $26.89
  range $12.87-$30.77, median ~$19.3, mean ~$20.8 (weight is a weak predictor: a 50 lb
  item quoted $26.89, a 1 lb item $16.69, a 6.2 lb item $30.77).
Action taken: golf listings now fetch the live per-item quote; fallback when a quote
cannot be obtained is $20.00 (measured median, rounded); other categories keep 13.50.

FINDING 3 - the bot only ever sees ShopGoodwill auctions in their final hour (the
closing-soon filter admits <=15 min contested / <=30 min uncontested), which is why
most observations sit at x1.00. Bid count was not recorded for ShopGoodwill rows, so
this analysis had to rebuild it from bid histories. It is now logged (bid_count).
