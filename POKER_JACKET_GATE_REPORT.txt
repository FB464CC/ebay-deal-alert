Uncertainty / tradeoff
----------------------
The 100-chip floor intentionally sacrifices alerts for rare and potentially valuable
single-chip/small collector lots. That is consistent with the complaint and the existing
search/research goal of finding a usable set, but it would be too strict if the desired goal
later changes to collecting individual casino chips.

The jacket heuristic is text-based. Suit chest/cut sizes and worsted/Super grades are strong
signals; pinstripe and especially herringbone can occasionally describe true casual
outerwear. Requiring the outerwear category, the literal word "jacket", and no trousers/set
signal limits that risk. It is still possible to suppress a genuine herringbone casual
jacket, but the current preference and disabled blazer/sport-coat searches favor rejecting
that ambiguity over repeating the incomplete-suit alert. Conversely, an unusually worded
suit jacket with none of the enumerated markers can still evade this heuristic.
