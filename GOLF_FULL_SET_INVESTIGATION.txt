  finish requests out of exact order this is a queue-pressure measure, not a
  claim that the same literal first 31 always finish, but it proves full-set
  golf is sometimes beyond the observed completion capacity and can be
  starved in a 31/94 deadline run.
- Shrinking the enabled-search count separately will reduce the queue directly.
  If ShopGoodwill's scheduled count falls to its reliably completed capacity

  modest reduction that leaves the queue above capacity improves odds but does
  not guarantee full-set coverage.

  move the same deadline starvation onto watches/poker and undo the existing
  cross-run rotation fairness; that is not a trivial report-only fix. The
  separately planned search-count reduction addresses the actual 94-request
  queue pressure without changing saved-search semantics here.
