Progress 3 - calibration decision
- Chrono24's current buyer guidance says that watch prices that appear too good to be true probably are, and eBay routes eligible watches through a physical Authenticity Guarantee inspection rather than treating listing photos or price as proof. This supports treating an unusually large claimed gap as a human-review warning, not as a bot authentication decision.
- Chosen boundary: 80% under the bot's estimated resale value. This is a deliberately bounded alert-only heuristic that catches all three reported 84-95% examples while leaving the existing 70% Steal threshold intact. It will not block a watch and it is not a claim that discounts below 80% are safe.
- For seller-description values, the conservative policy is to surface an explicit higher current-market/resale/appraisal figure as an unverified seller claim while retaining the AI's lower estimate for deal math. Automatically raising resale to a seller-authored number would make an exaggerated description manufacture a larger discount and a stronger Steal label.
